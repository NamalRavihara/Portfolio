Research & Project Experience

The Nesting Behaviour of Weaver Ants (Oecophylla smaragdina) in Relation to Tree Size

As part of our Ecology coursework, our group conducted a field-based mini research study examining how the nesting behavior of weaver ants correlates with the size of host trees near the Faculty of Applied Sciences.

Research Objectives:

* To observe and document nesting frequency, location, and structure of weaver ant colonies.

* To analyze whether tree height and canopy size influence the ants' nesting preferences.

* To understand how microhabitat selection by ants supports colony success and ecological adaptation.

Designing a Garlic Soy Sauce Production Facility – A Microbiological Perspective

As part of our Food Microbiology coursework, our group developed a conceptual layout and process flow design for a garlic soy sauce food factory. This project aimed to integrate microbiological safety, hygienic design, and efficient food processing principles.

Key highlights of the project:

* Created a detailed factory sketch illustrating raw material reception, fermentation tanks, mixing zones, bottling areas, and quality control sections.

* Incorporated critical control points (CCPs) based on HACCP principles to ensure microbial safety and product integrity.

* Considered contamination risks, proper zoning, and sanitation flow within the facility.

* Researched the microbiological aspects of soy sauce fermentation and the antimicrobial properties of garlic to inform our design decisions.
